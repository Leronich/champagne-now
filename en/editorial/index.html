Editorial Standards

Champagne.now is an independent cultural publication about the Champagne region. This page explains how we research, write, and verify the content we publish.

Who We Are

Champagne.now is an independent editorial project dedicated to the Champagne wine region — its terroir, history, houses, growers, and culture. We are not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to the Comité Champagne (CIVC), any Champagne house, or any official industry body.

All content is produced by the Champagne.now editorial team. We do not publish content from external contributors or sponsored posts without clear disclosure.

Fact-Checking

Every factual claim — production statistics, founding dates, geological facts, price ranges — is verified against primary sources before publication. Primary sources include CIVC official data, house documentation, BIVB records, and established wine reference works.

Where precise figures cannot be independently verified, we use qualified language: "approximately," "roughly," "according to house records," or "historians of the region note." We do not invent precision.

Prices are listed in USD and reflect market conditions at time of writing. Wine prices fluctuate — we recommend verifying current prices before purchase.

Affiliate Disclosure

Some articles contain affiliate links to hotels, tours, and wine retailers. When you purchase through these links, Champagne.now may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. Affiliate relationships do not influence our editorial decisions — we only recommend properties, experiences, and bottles we would recommend without compensation.

All affiliate links are identified with the labels "Stay Nearby," "Find This Bottle," or "Check Availability." For full details, see our Affiliate Disclosure.

Updates & Corrections

We update articles when significant new information becomes available — new vintage releases, changes in house ownership, revised CIVC classification data. The dateModified field in each article's structured data reflects the date of the most recent substantive update.

If you believe an article contains a factual error, contact us at contact page. Corrections are made promptly and noted where material.

Independence

No Champagne house, négociant, or tourism body pays for editorial coverage. Positive coverage of a producer does not follow from commercial relationships. Our sommelier recommendations reflect genuine assessment of quality and value.

Champagne.now is reader-supported through affiliate commissions and does not carry advertising.
